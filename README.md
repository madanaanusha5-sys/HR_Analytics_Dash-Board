# HR Analytics Dashboard — Employee Attrition Analysis


## Project Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition and workforce demographics using Microsoft Excel. An interactive-style dashboard summarizes key HR metrics and highlights employee groups with higher observed attrition rates.


## Objectives
Measure overall employee attrition.
Analyze attrition by department, age group, job role, and overtime status.
Summarize workforce metrics using KPI cards and charts.
Identify patterns that can support HR decision-making.
## Tools Used
Microsoft Excel for the web
Excel formulas
Excel tables and summary analysis
Charts and dashboard visualization
## Key Metrics
**Total Employees:** 1,470
**Active Employees:** 1,233
**Employees Who Left:** 237
**Overall Attrition Rate:** 16.12%
## Key Findings
**Age group:** Employees under 25 had the highest observed attrition rate at 39.18%.
**Job role:** Sales Representatives had the highest observed job-role attrition rate at 39.76%.
**Department:** Sales had a higher attrition rate (20.63%) than Research & Development (13.84%).
**Overtime:** Employees working overtime had a 30.53% attrition rate, compared with 10.44% for employees not working overtime.
## Business Recommendations
Explore retention and career-development opportunities for younger employees.
Review workload, onboarding, and retention practices for Sales Representatives.
Investigate factors associated with attrition in the Sales department.
Examine overtime patterns, employee feedback, and workload distribution.
These findings show associations in the dataset; they do not establish that any single factor causes attrition.
